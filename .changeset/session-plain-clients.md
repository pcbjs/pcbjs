Every client of the session connects to it: the session sends the editor events, `pcbjs:ide:boards`, `scopes`, `scope` and `log`, to each connection whose hello takes them, and the last ones again after a hello. A client detaches by closing its connection. A disk write under an editor's text that an agent claimed with `session/edited` names that agent in its shadowed `edit` event. `@pcbjs/core/protocol` holds the protocol versions.
