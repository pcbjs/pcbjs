The extension is a plain client of the session: it connects to the socket or pipe that `.pcbjs/dev.json` names, or the `/__pcbjs/session` WebSocket, starts `pcbjs session serve` only when no session is live, and connects again with a backoff when the connection is lost. A disk write under unsaved text is merged three ways, a clean merge applied with the cursor kept and a notice naming the writer, a conflict opened in the merge editor. The Boards view lists the git worktrees with their agents, boards and changed files, with Open diff, Open board and Merge, and the Agents view shows each agent's task, progress, changed files and findings delta, with Stop and tell, sent through `session/send` and shown as sent, read or returned; an agent's plan shows as a notification with Reply.
