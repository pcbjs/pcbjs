Editing polish: the views take only the keys of their table and every other chord, cmd or ctrl P among them, passes untouched, an editor's pane handing it to the editor. A committed drag stays where it went, half-opaque, until the compile that draws it there lands, never flickering back in 2D or 3D. A selected part, group or instance under a grid shows that grid as dots through its own origin and a move snaps to them. The rotation ring sits on the anchor's origin, an instance's its layout frame's, as a dark band with ticks every 15 degrees, majors every 90, and a heading triangle pointing in at the rotation. With an editor attached the chip has no Open placement or Open definition button: a click jumps to the placement and an alt-click to the definition, keeping the selection.
