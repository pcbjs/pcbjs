Assist mode in the 2D view: while the editor cursor waits in an empty chain call or `at=""`, the start pad gets a cursor at its centre and the view shows candidate dots, a line for `h` and `v`, four directions for `l`, a matrix for `x` and `at`, every grid dot with shift; they fall off from the anchor and the pointer, stay inside the outline, sit faint with the reason on another net's copper, and the pointer lights the grid around it. Hovering a dot, or typing the argument, previews the trace with a marching line and its clearance findings; a click writes the argument through the editor; Escape leaves.
