# Advanced

Choose a topic after completing [Blinky](../getting-started/). Each topic has focused lessons with a small example, an exercise and a result to inspect.

## Reusable design

- [Reusable blocks](./composition/reusable-blocks): define a circuit once and place several independent instances.
- [Instance and library layouts](./composition/instance-layouts): choose which instances a layout edit should affect.
- [Reference labels](./composition/reference-labels): position a label independently of its footprint.

## Precision placement

- [Placement grids](./precision/placement-grids): use a meaningful pitch and local origin.
- [Transforms and rotation](./precision/transforms): reason about rotation, translation and board side.

## Routing and copper

- [Route constraints](./routing/route-constraints): choose widths and clearances before routing.
- [Differential pairs](./routing/differential-pairs): route related signals as a pair.
- [Length matching](./routing/length-matching): set a routing objective and inspect whether it can be met.
- [Copper pours](./routing/copper-pours): fill an area with copper connected to a chosen net.

## Inspection and delivery

- [Part models](./delivery/part-models): align a model with its footprint and inspect the assembly.
- [Manufacturing output](./delivery/manufacturing): generate and review the fabrication set.

For exact syntax beyond a lesson, follow its Reference links. The guide explains a workflow; the [Reference](/docs/advanced) lists the full options.
