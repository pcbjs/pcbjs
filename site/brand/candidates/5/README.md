**5. Board corner.** The rounded corner of a board, bare FR-4 edge, a plated mounting hole and two traces leaving for the rest of the design. The first thing every board file draws: its outline and its holes.
Colours: dark mask #0F2219, green mask #1E6B46, bare FR-4 #D8C99B, ENIG gold #E8B84F, copper #D07A3C; Inter or the system sans for the wordmark.
