**1. Trace p.** A single 64-unit copper trace with 45 degree chamfers draws a lowercase p, and its descender lands on a gold via. One routed net is the whole letter, the way a pcbjs route is one call.
Colours: dark mask #0F2219, copper #D07A3C, ENIG gold #E8B84F; Inter or the system sans for the wordmark.
